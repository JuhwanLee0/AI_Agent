# 손그림 프롬프트 템플릿 모음 (Prompt Templates)

주요 AI 이미지 생성 도구(Midjourney, Google Whisk, DALL-E 3, Imagen 3)에서 최고의 결과물을 내는 표준 영문 프롬프트 구조입니다.

---

## 1. 표준 마스터 템플릿 (Master Template)

```text
A minimalist 16:9 editorial hand-drawn conceptual illustration on a pure clean white background.
[메인 피사체 / 메타포 장면 묘사].
Drawn in clean, loose, organic black ink pen line drawing with high aesthetic balance.
Selective minimal accent colors of vibrant coral red, warm orange, and soft cobalt blue.
Hand-written Korean/English annotations reading "[라벨1]", "[라벨2]" near the components.
Generous empty whitespace, thoughtful editorial style, conceptual book diagram aesthetic, 16:9 aspect ratio, no photorealism, no 3D rendering, no neon gradients.
```

---

## 2. 도구별 최적화 파라미터

### Midjourney v6 / Niji
```text
/imagine prompt: A minimalist 16:9 editorial hand-drawn conceptual illustration on a pure white background, [메타포 묘사], organic loose black ink pen line art, subtle coral red and cobalt blue accent color highlights, minimal handwritten text annotations, clean whitespace, magazine illustration style --ar 16:9 --style raw --v 6.0
```

### Google Labs Whisk / Nano Banana Pro
```text
A flat minimalist editorial hand-drawn conceptual diagram on a pure white background, [메타포 묘사], expressive black ink pen outlines, minimal accent pops of red and blue, clean whitespace, 16:9 aspect ratio, 2D line drawing aesthetic
```

### DALL-E 3 (ChatGPT)
```text
A 16:9 minimalist editorial hand-drawn illustration with abundant clean white background. In the center, [메타포 묘사]. The drawing uses simple black ink lines with a charming organic feel. There are small selective splashes of coral red and soft blue watercolor accents. Include neat hand-drawn text labels reading "[라벨1]" and "[라벨2]". Clean, thoughtful, and uncluttered.
```

## 3. 실전 예시 프롬프트

### 예시 A: 아이디어 압축과 정제 (The Idea Press)
- **English Prompt**:
  ```text
  A minimalist 16:9 editorial hand-drawn illustration on a pure white background. In the center, a simple mechanical press squeezing scattered rough idea notes (highlighted in soft orange) into a single glowing sharp crystal (highlighted in bright cobalt blue). Loose organic black ink pen drawing, hand-written text annotations reading "Raw Thoughts" on top and "Core Insight" at the bottom. Generous empty whitespace, conceptual book illustration aesthetic, 16:9 aspect ratio.
  ```

### 예시 B: 두 부서 간의 안전한 인수인계 (The Handoff Bridge)
- **English Prompt**:
  ```text
  A minimalist 16:9 editorial hand-drawn illustration on a pure white background. Two cliff edges connected by a sturdy hand-drawn wooden suspension bridge with soft blue cables. A small stick-like character passes a glowing warm orange document packet to another character in the middle. Hand-written Korean labels reading "기획" on the left and "개발" on the right. Clean black ink lines, ample whitespace, 16:9 aspect ratio.
  ```
